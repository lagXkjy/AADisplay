# Privacy — Community Fork R1.0

Community Fork R1.0 does not provide its own account system and does not intentionally collect telemetry on behalf of this fork.

The Phone Input Bridge processes entered text locally on the device in order to send it to the selected AADisplay pane. This fork does not intentionally upload that input text to a project-owned server.

AADisplay runs alongside Android Auto, Android system components and user-selected third-party applications. Those components may have their own network activity, data collection and privacy policies. Their behavior is outside the scope of this fork's privacy statement.

When sharing logs in GitHub Issues, remove personal data such as phone numbers, contact names, account identifiers, notification contents and location information.
